Edge-native

Self-healing mesh

Systems engineering

The enclosure, radios and rules carry the signal.

Each pillar connects physical design to what happens on your floor.

  1. 01

    Radio path

    Twin articulated antennas and a clear RF window keep links strong through racks, ducts and walls.

    Coverage starts with the body.

  2. 02

    Local control

    Thresholds, interlocks and alarms execute on the gateway itself, in the same room as the failure.

    You shouldn't need the cloud to stop a fault.

  3. 03

    Facility sealing

    The shell, ports and mounts are planned around dust, heat and cleaning cycles.

    Rugged without looking industrial.
