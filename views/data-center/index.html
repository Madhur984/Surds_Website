Follow the power

From the grid to every server

Power takes the same route every second of every day. If any link fails, the next one is already running.

  1. 1Grid inThe utility feed arrives and transformers step it down.
  2. 2GeneratorsIf the grid fails, they start and take the load in about 10 seconds.
  3. 3SwitchgearPicks grid or generator, automatically.
  4. 4UPS and batteriesCarry every rack through those 10 seconds. Two systems, A and B.
  5. 5BuswayTwo separate feeds run over every row.
  6. 6RacksEach server has two power supplies, one on each feed.
Follow the heat

Every watt becomes heat

Servers turn almost all their power into heat. Water carries it up to the roof and comes back cold.

  1. 1ServersHeat leaves the back of every rack into the hot aisle.
  2. 2CRAH unitsHall air passes over cold-water coils and goes back under the floor, cool.
  3. 3Warm water risesThe orange riser carries the heat up through the building.
  4. 4Roof plantFans push the heat into the outside air.
  5. 5ChillersOn the hottest days they chill the water before it goes back down (blue).
Follow the data

Where the internet comes in

Fibre from the outside world lands in one room, then fans out to every rack in the building.

  1. 1CarriersInternet providers hand over their fibre in locked cages.
  2. 2Patch framesEvery link ends here as a labelled jumper.
  3. 3Core routersThe backbone: they move traffic in and out of the building.
  4. 4AI racksGPU racks get the fastest links on the network floor.
  5. 5Server rowsYellow trays carry fibre up to every row in the data hall.
